Privacy Policy

Permission Compare for Salesforce (Chrome extension) · Last updated: November 4, 2026
Independent tool. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Salesforce, Inc. Salesforce is a trademark of Salesforce, Inc.

1. Salesforce session (authentication information)

The extension reads the sid session cookie for Salesforce orgs on *.my.salesforce.com where you are already logged in in this browser. The session ID is kept in memory only while the extension page is open. It is used only to call your own org's Salesforce REST API directly from your browser. It is never stored, logged, or sent to the developer or any third party.

2. Salesforce data

User names, usernames, emails, profiles, permission sets, and object and field permissions are read from your org and processed locally in your browser to build the comparison. None of this data is uploaded anywhere. CSV and Excel exports are created locally and saved to your computer.

3. Feedback (personal communications and optional email)

Data is sent only when you type feedback and click Send. The feedback text, your email (optional), and the extension version are then sent to Formspree (Formspree, Inc.), which processes form submissions for us and forwards them to the developer by email. See the Formspree Privacy Policy. Your email is optional and used only to reply to your feedback. It is never used for marketing, and it is never sold or shared.

4. Retention and deletion

The extension itself stores nothing persistently: closing the tab discards everything. Feedback submissions are kept in the developer's Formspree account and mailbox for up to 12 months and are then deleted. To have your feedback deleted sooner, email the contact address below. We will delete it from Formspree and from our mailbox within 30 days.

5. Analytics and tracking

None. The extension contains no analytics, ads, or remote code.
