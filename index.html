Permission Compare for Salesforce

Independent tool. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Salesforce, Inc.

A free Chrome extension that compares two users' effective permissions side by side. It works within one org or across orgs, for example production vs sandbox.

  • System, object (CRUD, View All, Modify All), and field (Read/Edit) permissions
  • The source of every grant: Profile, Permission Set, or Permission Set Group
  • Cross-org compare with "Not in org" markers and auto-matching of sandbox users
  • Export to CSV or Excel
  • Your data stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
